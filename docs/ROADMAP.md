# Sevenbound Roadmap

## Prototype goals already represented

- Seven-slot bracelet monster storage
- Bracelet upgrades with coins and relics
- Normal and charged bracelet attacks
- Monster taming after weakening HP
- Nexarion Equinox apex mission
- Bracelet monster recovery
- Lab NPCs, research console, and people-helped progress
- Lost diaries, wall inscriptions, and survivor notes
- Random islands, weather, biomes, and broken homes

## Suggested next milestone: full engine version

Move the prototype into Godot, Unity, or Unreal to support:

- True 3D movement and camera
- Higher quality 3D island environments
- Animation state machines for monsters
- Dialogue trees with NPC portraits
- Inventory and crafting UI
- Expanded lab rooms and research terminals
- Saving through local files instead of browser storage
- Steam Input action names
- Steam Deck verification pass

## Suggested production slices

### Slice 1: Island traversal

Build one complete island with broken homes, ruins, hazards, crates, weather, and extraction shield.

### Slice 2: Monster combat

Create 10 monster families with 3 forms each, automatic skill logic, recovery, and taming.

### Slice 3: Lab loop

Build decontamination, research console, NPC dialogue, reserve specimen storage, and disease-cure progress.

### Slice 4: Tournament mode

Create controlled arenas where the player tests the seven-monster team outside survival islands.
